Privacy Policy

ANDROID SPYWARE DETECTION

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: Android Spyware Detection does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. Everything the app checks stays on your phone.

1. What the app does

Android Spyware Detection helps you find signs of spyware on your Android phone, lets you block chosen apps from using the internet (firewall), and creates PDF security reports.

2. Information the app reads on your phone

To check your phone, the app reads the following only on your device:

  • List of installed apps and the permissions they have (to find spy apps, risky permissions and tracking code).
  • Device details such as phone model, Android version, security patch date, battery and storage status.
  • Accessibility services and device admin apps that are turned on (common signs of spyware).
  • Recently used apps, only if you choose to allow Usage Access.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Firewall (VPN)

The firewall uses Android's VpnService to create a local VPN on your phone. It only receives traffic from the apps you choose to block, and drops that traffic so those apps cannot reach the internet. The VPN does not send your traffic to any server, does not read the content of your communication, and does not collect browsing data. The firewall log (which blocked app tried to connect, and to which address) is kept only in the app's memory on your phone.

4. PDF reports

Reports are saved privately inside the app on your phone. They are only shared if you press Share and choose where to send them. You can delete them at any time.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data we collect or share

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking code.
  • No account or login is needed.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
